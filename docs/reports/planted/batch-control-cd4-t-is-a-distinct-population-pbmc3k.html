Methods

Raw counts for 2,700 cells and 32,738 genes were taken from layers['counts'] of the input file (SHA-256 1ba564ee0664…). Each of 24 pipeline runs (1 baseline, 17 one-at-a-time variations and 6 seeded random combinations) re-derived clusters from these counts. Cells were kept if they expressed at least {100, 200*, 500} genes and had at most {5.0, 10.0*, 20.0}% mitochondrial counts; genes detected in fewer than 3 cells were dropped. Doublets were scored with Scrublet (scanpy implementation, seed 0) and removed when the doublet_removal axis was on (baseline: on). Expression was normalised either by total-count scaling to 10,000 counts per cell followed by log1p and z-scaling of {1000, 2000*, 3000} highly variable genes (Seurat flavour), or by analytic Pearson residuals on the same number of genes (baseline: log_total). {20, 30*, 50} principal components (ARPACK, seeded) were computed, optionally corrected for the batch column `batch` with Harmony (baseline: on); a 15-nearest-neighbour graph (exact search) was clustered with Leiden (igraph flavour, resolution {0.3, 0.5, 1.0*, 1.5, 2.0}, seeds {0*, 1, 2}). The target population was located in each run as the union of clusters (at least 10 cells) in which at least 50% of cells carry the label `CD4 T` in `tool_label`. Overlap with the reference cell set (cells labelled `CD4 T` in `tool_label` (728 cells)) was measured by the Jaccard index over barcodes, counting cells removed by QC as absent.
