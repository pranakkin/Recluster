Methods

Raw counts for 2,700 cells and 32,738 genes were taken from layers['counts'] of the input file (SHA-256 a2e28d1fd201…). Each of 24 pipeline runs (1 baseline, 17 one-at-a-time variations and 6 seeded random combinations) re-derived clusters from these counts. Cells were kept if they expressed at least {100, 200*, 500} genes and had at most {5.0, 10.0*, 20.0}% mitochondrial counts; a threshold that alone would remove more than 25% of the cells was pulled back to the value that removes exactly that fraction, and the run records both values. Genes detected in fewer than 3 cells were dropped. Doublets were scored with Scrublet (scanpy implementation, seed 0, exact neighbours); when its automatic threshold called fewer than 25% of the expected doublets (0.8% per 1,000 cells) the top expected fraction by score was called instead. Predicted doublets were removed when the doublet_removal axis was on (baseline: on). In those runs, after clustering, every cluster of at least 10 cells was compared with simulated doublets of two other cell-type groups (the other clusters grouped by the correlation of their mean expression; each doublet sums the raw counts of one random cell from each group, for the 3 pairs of groups that best explain the cluster's mean expression), and a cluster that a cross-validated logistic-regression classifier could not separate from them (out-of-fold AUROC, mean of 5 repetitions, below 0.80 for some pair) was removed as a doublet population. Expression was normalised either by total-count scaling to 10,000 counts per cell followed by log1p and z-scaling of {1000, 2000*, 3000} highly variable genes (Seurat flavour), or by analytic Pearson residuals on the same number of genes (baseline: log_total). {20, 30*, 50} principal components (ARPACK, seeded) were computed, optionally corrected for the batch column `batch` with Harmony (baseline: on); a 15-nearest-neighbour graph (exact search) was clustered with Leiden (igraph flavour, resolution {0.3, 0.5, 1.0*, 1.5, 2.0}, seeds {0*, 1, 2}). The target population was located in each run by label overlap: a cluster of at least 10 cells qualified when at least 50% of its cells carry `CD4 T` in `tool_label`; the matched population is the union of qualifying clusters. Overlap with the reference cell set (cells labelled `CD4 T` in `tool_label` (670 cells)) was measured by the Jaccard index over barcodes that passed the run's QC; the fraction of reference cells removed by QC is reported alongside. The claim held in a run when the matched population had at least 20 cells, Jaccard >= 0.5.
