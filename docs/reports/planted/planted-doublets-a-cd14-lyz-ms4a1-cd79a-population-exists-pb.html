Methods

Raw counts for 2,850 cells and 32,738 genes were taken from .X of the input file (SHA-256 e2413f00535e…). Each of 23 pipeline runs (1 baseline, 16 one-at-a-time variations and 6 seeded random combinations) re-derived clusters from these counts. Cells were kept if they expressed at least {100, 200*, 500} genes and had at most {5.0, 10.0*, 20.0}% mitochondrial counts; genes detected in fewer than 3 cells were dropped. Doublets were scored with Scrublet (scanpy implementation, seed 0) and removed when the doublet_removal axis was on (baseline: on). Expression was normalised either by total-count scaling to 10,000 counts per cell followed by log1p and z-scaling of {1000, 2000*, 3000} highly variable genes (Seurat flavour), or by analytic Pearson residuals on the same number of genes (baseline: log_total). {20, 30*, 50} principal components (ARPACK, seeded) were computed; a 15-nearest-neighbour graph (exact search) was clustered with Leiden (igraph flavour, resolution {0.3, 0.5, 1.0*, 1.5, 2.0}, seeds {0*, 1, 2}). The target population was located in each run by marker enrichment: a per-cell score (mean CP10k-log1p expression of CD14, LYZ, MS4A1, CD79A, z-scored) was computed once from the raw counts, and clusters with at least 10 cells whose AUROC for that score versus all other cells was at least 0.8 were taken as the population. Overlap with the reference cell set (population found in the baseline run (952 cells)) was measured by the Jaccard index over barcodes, counting cells removed by QC as absent. The claim held in a run when the matched population had at least 20 cells, Jaccard >= 0.5, marker AUROC >= 0.8.
