Methods

Raw counts for 2,850 cells and 32,738 genes were taken from .X of the input file (SHA-256 ff1e67cd1414…). Each of 23 pipeline runs (1 baseline, 16 one-at-a-time variations and 6 seeded random combinations) re-derived clusters from these counts. Cells were kept if they expressed at least {100, 200*, 500} genes and had at most {5.0, 10.0*, 20.0}% mitochondrial counts; a threshold that alone would remove more than 25% of the cells was pulled back to the value that removes exactly that fraction, and the run records both values. Genes detected in fewer than 3 cells were dropped. Doublets were scored with Scrublet (scanpy implementation, seed 0, exact neighbours); when its automatic threshold called fewer than 25% of the expected doublets (0.8% per 1,000 cells) the top expected fraction by score was called instead. Predicted doublets were removed when the doublet_removal axis was on (baseline: on). In those runs, after clustering, every cluster of at least 10 cells was compared with simulated doublets of two other cell-type groups (the other clusters grouped by the correlation of their mean expression; each doublet sums the raw counts of one random cell from each group, for the 3 pairs of groups that best explain the cluster's mean expression), and a cluster that a cross-validated logistic-regression classifier could not separate from them (out-of-fold AUROC, mean of 5 repetitions, below 0.80 for some pair) was removed as a doublet population. Expression was normalised either by total-count scaling to 10,000 counts per cell followed by log1p and z-scaling of {1000, 2000*, 3000} highly variable genes (Seurat flavour), or by analytic Pearson residuals on the same number of genes (baseline: log_total). {20, 30*, 50} principal components (ARPACK, seeded) were computed; a 15-nearest-neighbour graph (exact search) was clustered with Leiden (igraph flavour, resolution {0.3, 0.5, 1.0*, 1.5, 2.0}, seeds {0*, 1, 2}). The target population was located in each run by marker enrichment: a per-cell score (the mean CP10k-log1p expression of GNLY, NKG7, CD14, LYZ, z-scored) was computed once from the raw counts, and a cluster of at least 10 cells qualified when its AUROC for that score was at least 0.8 against all other cells and more than 50% of the markers were individually enriched (per-marker AUROC at least 0.6); the matched population is the union of qualifying clusters. Overlap with the reference cell set (population found in run 18 (101 cells)) was measured by the Jaccard index over barcodes that passed the run's QC; the fraction of reference cells removed by QC is reported alongside.
