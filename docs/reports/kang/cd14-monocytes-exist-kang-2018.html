Methods

Raw counts for 24,673 cells and 15,706 genes were taken from .X of the input file (SHA-256 e6a5adac64dc…). Each of 21 pipeline runs (1 baseline, 14 one-at-a-time variations and 6 seeded random combinations) re-derived clusters from these counts. Cells were kept if they expressed at least {100, 200*, 500} genes (the input has no mitochondrial genes, so the mitochondrial-fraction axis was not varied); a threshold that alone would remove more than 25% of the cells was pulled back to the value that removes exactly that fraction, and the run records both values. Genes detected in fewer than 3 cells were dropped. Doublets were scored with Scrublet (scanpy implementation, per sample, seed 0, exact neighbours); when its automatic threshold called fewer than 25% of the expected doublets (0.8% per 1,000 cells) the top expected fraction by score was called instead. Predicted doublets were removed when the doublet_removal axis was on (baseline: on). Expression was normalised either by total-count scaling to 10,000 counts per cell followed by log1p and z-scaling of {1000, 2000*, 3000} highly variable genes (Seurat flavour), or by analytic Pearson residuals on the same number of genes (baseline: log_total). {20, 30*, 50} principal components (ARPACK, seeded) were computed; a 15-nearest-neighbour graph (exact search) was clustered with Leiden (igraph flavour, resolution {0.3, 0.5, 1.0*, 1.5, 2.0}, seeds {0*, 1, 2}). The target population was located in each run by marker enrichment: a per-cell score (the mean CP10k-log1p expression of CD14, LYZ, S100A8, z-scored) was computed once from the raw counts, and a cluster of at least 10 cells qualified when its AUROC for that score was at least 0.8 within samples (a stratified within-sample AUROC: the Mann-Whitney U statistics of all samples pooled, so that the mix of other cell types a QC setting keeps cannot move it) and more than 50% of the markers were individually enriched (per-marker AUROC at least 0.6) or by label overlap: a cluster of at least 10 cells qualified when at least 50% of its cells carry `CD14+ Monocytes` in `cell_type`; the matched population is the union of qualifying clusters. Overlap with the reference cell set (cells labelled `CD14+ Monocytes` in `cell_type` (5697 cells)) was measured by the Jaccard index over barcodes that passed the run's QC; the fraction of reference cells removed by QC is reported alongside. The claim held in a run when the matched population had at least 20 cells, Jaccard >= 0.5, marker AUROC >= 0.8, and at least 5 cells in each of at least 2 samples.
