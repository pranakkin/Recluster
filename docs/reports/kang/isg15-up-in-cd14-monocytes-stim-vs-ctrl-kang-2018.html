Methods

Raw counts for 24,673 cells and 15,706 genes were taken from .X of the input file (SHA-256 e6a5adac64dc…). Each of 21 pipeline runs (1 baseline, 14 one-at-a-time variations and 6 seeded random combinations) re-derived clusters from these counts. Cells were kept if they expressed at least {100, 200*, 500} genes (the input has no mitochondrial genes, so the mitochondrial-fraction axis was not varied); genes detected in fewer than 3 cells were dropped. Doublets were scored with Scrublet (scanpy implementation, per sample, seed 0) and removed when the doublet_removal axis was on (baseline: on). Expression was normalised either by total-count scaling to 10,000 counts per cell followed by log1p and z-scaling of {1000, 2000*, 3000} highly variable genes (Seurat flavour), or by analytic Pearson residuals on the same number of genes (baseline: log_total). {20, 30*, 50} principal components (ARPACK, seeded) were computed; a 15-nearest-neighbour graph (exact search) was clustered with Leiden (igraph flavour, resolution {0.3, 0.5, 1.0*, 1.5, 2.0}, seeds {0*, 1, 2}). The target population was located in each run by marker enrichment: a per-cell score (mean CP10k-log1p expression of CD14, LYZ, S100A8, z-scored) was computed once from the raw counts, and clusters with at least 10 cells whose AUROC for that score versus all other cells was at least 0.8 were taken as the population. Overlap with the reference cell set (cells labelled `CD14+ Monocytes` in `cell_type` (5697 cells)) was measured by the Jaccard index over barcodes, counting cells removed by QC as absent. For each run, raw counts of cells in the population were summed per sample (`replicate`, `label`; samples with fewer than 5 cells dropped) and tested for `stim` versus `ctrl` with DESeq2 (pydeseq2, design ~condition, genes with >= 10 total counts). P-values for the 1 claimed gene(s) were Benjamini-Hochberg adjusted over the claimed genes (genome-wide adjusted values are also shown). The claim held when every claimed gene had adjusted p < 0.05 with direction `up_in_a`. A cell-level two-sided Wilcoxon rank-sum test on CP10k-log1p values is reported alongside; a gene significant only at the cell level is flagged as likely pseudoreplication (Squair et al., Nat Commun 2021). Verdicts: ROBUST if the claim held in at least 80% of runs, FRAGILE if in at least 20%, otherwise NOT SUPPORTED. All thresholds are listed below and are configurable.
