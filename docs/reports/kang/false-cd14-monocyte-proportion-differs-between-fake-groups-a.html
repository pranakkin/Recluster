Methods

Raw counts for 12,315 cells and 15,706 genes were taken from .X of the input file (SHA-256 56873b6d08d3…). Each of 21 pipeline runs (1 baseline, 14 one-at-a-time variations and 6 seeded random combinations) re-derived clusters from these counts. Cells were kept if they expressed at least {100, 200*, 500} genes (the input has no mitochondrial genes, so the mitochondrial-fraction axis was not varied); genes detected in fewer than 3 cells were dropped. Doublets were scored with Scrublet (scanpy implementation, per sample, seed 0) and removed when the doublet_removal axis was on (baseline: on). Expression was normalised either by total-count scaling to 10,000 counts per cell followed by log1p and z-scaling of {1000, 2000*, 3000} highly variable genes (Seurat flavour), or by analytic Pearson residuals on the same number of genes (baseline: log_total). {20, 30*, 50} principal components (ARPACK, seeded) were computed; a 15-nearest-neighbour graph (exact search) was clustered with Leiden (igraph flavour, resolution {0.3, 0.5, 1.0*, 1.5, 2.0}, seeds {0*, 1, 2}). The target population was located in each run by marker enrichment: a per-cell score (mean CP10k-log1p expression of CD14, LYZ, S100A8, z-scored) was computed once from the raw counts, and clusters with at least 10 cells whose AUROC for that score versus all other cells was at least 0.8 were taken as the population. Overlap with the reference cell set (cells labelled `CD14+ Monocytes` in `cell_type` (2932 cells)) was measured by the Jaccard index over barcodes, counting cells removed by QC as absent. For each run the proportion of cells in the population was computed per sample (`replicate`, `fake_group`), and samples in `A` were compared with samples in `B` by a Welch t-test on logit-transformed proportions, so that the biological replicate, not the cell, is the unit of inference. The claim held when p < 0.05 and the direction was `different`. A cell-level chi-square test on pooled cells is reported only to show the effect of pseudoreplication.
